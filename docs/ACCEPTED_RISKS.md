## Rules

1. Every CRITICAL/HIGH entry must have a named owner and review date.
2. A lapsed review date blocks release until re-reviewed or resolved.
3. Acknowledging a risk does NOT remove it from the governance report — it adds
   the evidence of review so the finding is "accepted" not "ignored".
